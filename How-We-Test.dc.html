Process

How we test

The methodology sets the criteria; this is the process our analyst actually runs against each company before it's scored.

  1. Public-record pass

    Pull every public certification, analyst placement and CVE credit for the vendor from primary registries — not the vendor's own marketing claims.

  2. Documentation review

    Read the vendor's own technical and compliance documentation to confirm the delivery model and domain coverage they claim matches what's actually shipped.

  3. Case-study verification

    Any client reference or case study cited must be independently findable — a press release, a named testimonial or a public filing, not an unverifiable anecdote.

  4. Weighted scoring

    Each of the five methodology criteria is scored 0–10 and combined by its published weight into the final score shown on the ranking page.

  5. Quarterly recheck

    Every listed company is re-verified each quarter; a material change (a lost certification, a new analyst placement) can move a company's tier immediately.
