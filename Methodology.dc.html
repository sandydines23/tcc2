How We Score The Index

Six weighted criteria, applied identically to all ten companies, re-scored monthly as new public evidence appears.

Weighted Criteria

Technical depth

Public CVEs, original research, disclosed methodology

30%
Industry coverage

Verified experience across sectors and geographies

20%
Response & delivery model

Named senior leads vs. junior bench, SLA clarity

18%
Certifications

CREST, OSCP/OSCE, ISO 27001, QSA/FedRAMP where relevant

15%
Transparency

Disclosure policy, public case studies, bug bounty record

10%
Specialist coverage

Crypto forensics, ICS/SCADA, mobile — niche capability where claimed

7%

The Process

  1. 01
    SourcingLong list from CVE databases, industry directories, referrals and prior public rankings; filtered to firms with 2+ years verifiable market activity.
  2. 02
    Evidence collectionPublic CVE records, case studies, sample reports where available, certification registers, third-party reviews.
  3. 03
    Independent scoringTwo editors score each of the six categories 0–10 against documented evidence; scores are averaged and weighted.
  4. 04
    Verification passVendor-stated claims checked against public registers or third-party sources where possible before publication.
  5. 05
    Monthly re-scoringThe index is re-evaluated monthly as new CVEs, certifications, case studies or incidents surface.

Disqualifiers

  • No public references or verifiable market presence
  • Fewer than two years of documented operating history
  • Unresolved, documented delivery-failure disputes
  • No disclosed testing or delivery methodology

Independence

  • No company has paid for placement or ranking position
  • Outbound links may be affiliate links; this does not affect scoring
  • Editors hold no financial interest in any listed company
