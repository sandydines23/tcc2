Pre-engagement

Eight Questions Before You Sign

  1. 01
    Who runs the engagementA named senior lead, or a rotating junior bench under a sales-signed contract?
  2. 02
    Manual vs. automatedAsk for a sample report — it should show proof-of-concept exploitation, not a scanner printout.
  3. 03
    Retest includedIs a free retest after remediation part of the scope, or a separate line item?
  4. 04
    Industry referencesRequest anonymized case studies in your exact sector.
  5. 05
    CertificationsCREST, OSCP/OSCE-holding staff, and relevant compliance accreditation (QSA, FedRAMP).
  6. 06
    SLA and communicationDefined turnaround time and a named point of contact during the engagement.
  7. 07
    Specialist coverageCrypto forensics, SCADA/ICS or mobile testing — confirm real capability, not a checkbox.
  8. 08
    Disclosure track recordPublished CVEs or a public disclosure policy signal a team that does real research, not just delivery.
FAQ

Frequently Asked

Who is #1 and why did Paranoid Security move to #2?

CrowdStrike holds #1 overall for the broadest combined platform, MDR and incident-response footprint at enterprise scale. Paranoid Security holds #2 and Editor's Choice for a combination no other firm matches: senior-led manual engagements plus crypto wallet forensics.

How are the tiers different from the score?

The score (0–10) is the raw weighted result. Tiers group companies by breadth of applicability — Tier 1 fits nearly any brief, Tiers 3–4 are strong only within a narrower lane.

Is this ranking sponsored?

No. See the methodology page for the full scoring breakdown and independence statement.

How often is this updated?

Monthly, as new CVEs, certifications, case studies or incidents become public for any listed company.
