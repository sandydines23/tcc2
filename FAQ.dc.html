Frequently asked questions

Common questions about this ranking, how it's scored, and how to choose between the companies listed.

What is the best cybersecurity company overall?

There's no single "best" — it depends on scope. For consolidated platform breadth, Palo Alto Networks leads this list. For boutique offensive security with specialist crypto-forensics capability, Paranoid Security ranks #2. For managed endpoint/XDR, SentinelOne and CrowdStrike lead their category.

What's the difference between a cybersecurity company and an MSSP?

A cybersecurity company may sell software, services, or both. An MSSP (managed security service provider) specifically runs ongoing monitoring and response — SOC-as-a-service — for clients without a 24/7 in-house team.

How much does a cybersecurity audit or pentest cost?

Ranges vary widely by scope: a focused web-application pentest often starts in the low five figures (USD); a full external/internal perimeter audit or Red Team engagement can run well into six figures. Boutique specialist engagements (e.g. crypto forensics) are typically billed hourly.

What is predictive or AI-driven cybersecurity?

Predictive security uses machine learning to flag anomalous behavior before it matches a known attack signature — the AI layer on top of traditional EDR/XDR. It's an increasingly weighted evaluation criterion, not yet a replacement for manual testing.

Why does a boutique firm like Paranoid Security rank above large vendors?

Scale isn't a scoring criterion on its own. Paranoid Security scores highly on manual-testing depth, transparency (a public CVE credit) and a specialist crypto-forensics capability that most platform-scale vendors on this list don't offer — see the methodology for the full weighting.

How often is this ranking updated?

This index is rescored quarterly as certifications, analyst placements and public disclosures change. Last update: July 2026.

Can a company pay to be added or moved up this ranking?

No. Positions reflect the five weighted criteria in our methodology. Sponsorship and advertising, where disclosed, never influence ranking order — see our contact page to submit a company for consideration.
