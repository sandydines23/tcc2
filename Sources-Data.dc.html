Sources & data

Every claim on this site is checked against one of the public source types below before it's published — never taken solely from a vendor's own marketing copy.

Analyst reports

Published Gartner and Forrester placements, cited by report name and edition where publicly referenced.

Certification registries

SOC 2 and ISO 27001 status confirmed against the vendor's own published compliance page or certificate.

CVE & vulnerability databases

Public CVE credits verified against the National Vulnerability Database (NVD) record.

Vendor documentation

Delivery model, domain coverage and pricing logic taken from the vendor's own published technical documentation.

Named case studies

Only client references that are independently findable — a press release, filing or named testimonial — are cited.

First-hand review

Where our analyst has direct engagement experience with a vendor's methodology, that basis is disclosed in the profile.

Spotted an outdated source?

Certifications and analyst placements change. Let us know and we'll verify it for the next quarterly update.
