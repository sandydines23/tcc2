About this index

Built To Be Checked, Not Trusted On Faith

Cybersecurity Rating reviews ten cybersecurity companies, not a hundred — every score traces back to public evidence, and the weighting is published, not hidden behind a "proprietary algorithm."

Our mission

Most vendor "top lists" in security are pay-to-play. We built this the way we'd want a security report written: fixed methodology, public criteria, no room to move a listing for the right invoice.

  • Every score traces to public evidence — CVEs, case studies, certifications
  • Scoring weights are published on the methodology page
  • The index is re-scored monthly as new evidence appears

Who this is for

Security leads, founders and compliance teams shortlisting a cybersecurity partner without weeks of vendor-by-vendor vetting.

  • Read the index for a fast shortlist
  • Read the methodology for the full scoring breakdown
  • Use the contact page to flag a correction or suggest a company
