Reading the matrix

What each column means

Domains covered

The security disciplines the vendor genuinely operates in — not just resells. A platform leader spans four or more; a specialist may cover one deeply.

Delivery model

Software you run yourself, a managed service (MDR/MSSP) that runs it for you, or a hybrid. This drives both cost structure and how much control you keep.

SOC 2 / ISO 27001

Third-party audited controls at the vendor itself — relevant when their platform will touch your regulated data or infrastructure.
